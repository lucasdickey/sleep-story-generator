Samir and Flicker continue their journey, their steps unhurried. … The desert is a place of patience … where time moves like the grains of sand … slowly … deliberately. … …

Flicker curls beside him, his presence a comforting weight … a reminder that in this wide world, companionship is a gift … a gentle anchor. … …
The desert holds its breath … the night wrapping around them like a familiar story … told and retold through the ages. … …
Samir closes his eyes … the soft hum of the night singing him into dreams. … Dreams of sweeping sands and gentle winds … of the elder's stories weaving through the fabric of the stars … of the quiet courage that walks beside him as constant as Flicker's footsteps. … …
